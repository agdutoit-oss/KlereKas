    Photo shoot

    Before you start
    • Phone at waist height, about 2.5 m away. A tripod, or the phone propped on a shelf or a stack of books.
    • Plain, light wall and even daylight. No window behind you, and no strong shadows.
    • Fitted clothes in a neutral colour. A snug t-shirt with leggings or cycling shorts, bare feet, hair tied back.
    • Same spot, same stance. Arms slightly away from your body, feet hip-width apart. Only turn between shots.
    • Go hands-free. The timer counts down and a voice tells you when to turn, so you never walk back to the phone.
    • For close-ups, move the phone to face height about an arm's length away, and keep your shoulders square.
